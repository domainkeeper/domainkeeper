> **I imagine it first. Then I figure out how to build it.**

I'm **Pratham**, a B.Tech CSE / AI-ML student exploring the intersection of **software development, artificial intelligence, and creative technology**.

I enjoy taking ideas that exist only as concepts in my head and turning them into something tangible.

I naturally think visually — often imagining an interface, system, interaction, or experience before I know exactly how I'm going to implement it.

I'm not an expert.

I'm not trying to look like one.

I'm learning, experimenting, building, breaking things, and getting better at understanding why they broke.
