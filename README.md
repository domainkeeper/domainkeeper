# About Me

I’m someone who enjoys turning imagination into reality through technology, creativity, and experimentation. I have a deep interest in art, visual thinking, and building ideas inspired by the things around me. I naturally think in visuals and concepts, often imagining systems, designs, and solutions in a highly spatial and “3D” way before bringing them into implementation.

I have foundational experience with programming languages such as Python, C++, and JavaScript, and I’m continuously working toward becoming a stronger and more capable developer. While I may still be growing technically, I value creativity, curiosity, and the willingness to keep learning through building real projects.

I enjoy exploring ambitious ideas, prototyping concepts, and collaborating with people who are passionate about creating meaningful things. Many of my experiments fail — but for me, every failed attempt is still part of the process of improving, learning, and refining better ideas.

Beyond coding, I’m highly inspired by aesthetics, storytelling, design, and innovation. My goal is not just to write code, but to create experiences, systems, and projects that feel alive and impactful.

Currently focusing on:

* AI & Future Technologies

* Open Collaboration and Learning

I believe consistency is something I still struggle with at times, especially with procrastination, but I’m actively working on becoming more disciplined and execution-focused every day.
